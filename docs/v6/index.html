Clinical Robot Competition v0.6.0

A 4-station clinical robot competition where Unitree G1 humanoid robots (from unitreerobotics) serve as doctors and nurses competing to perform patient injection procedures on a realistic human patient. Each station runs independently with PPO reinforcement learning policies.

Unitree G1 Robots

  • 1.32m tall, 35kg, 23–43 degrees of freedom
  • Dark charcoal body, glossy visor head, metallic joints
  • Dex3-1 three-fingered dexterous hands
  • Doctor G1: white medical coat, red cross, holds syringe
  • Nurse G1: blue medical coat, gold badge, holds tablet

How the Simulation Works

  • Stations: 4 identical stations (A, B, C, D) in a 2x2 grid
  • Policy: All stations use the same PPO architecture (MLP 64x64, tanh activation)
  • State: Each station has a unique random seed (42, 137, 256, 512)
  • Reward: R = −0.3 × time + 0.5/(1+dist) + 0.2/(1+jerk)
  • Phases: 7 phases; PPO only affects phase durations

Why Robot Competitions for Oncology

Having multiple autonomous robots compete simultaneously on injection procedures demonstrates that parallel robot trials can accelerate clinical workflows compared to sequential human trials, making future fully autonomous physical AI oncology trials faster.

Competition Ranking

  • Time: Total elapsed seconds for all 7 phases
  • Accuracy: Needle-to-target distance. Score = 1/(1+dist*100)
  • Winner: Fastest total time, tiebroken by accuracy
