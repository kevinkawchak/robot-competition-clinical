Clinical Robot Competition v1.0.0 — GCP Trial Suite

Four autonomous stations compete to execute a complete, GCP-aligned intramuscular injection visit on realistic human trial participants. Each station is staffed by two Unitree G1 humanoid robots (43 DOF incl. Dex3-1 dexterous hands): a doctor G1 (white coat) and a nurse G1 (scrub-blue coat). A human Clinical Research Associate (CRA) observes from the sponsor monitoring desk. The room is open-top (no ceiling) and every part of it can be inspected: zoom is cursor-targeted down to 5 cm, and double-clicking any object glides the camera to it.

12-Phase GCP Workflow (per station)

  1. Identity verification — nurse scans wristband (two identifiers)
  2. eConsent confirmation — nurse presents tablet, participant nods
  3. Baseline vitals — nurse captures ECG/SpO2/BP on live monitor
  4. Hand hygiene & gloves — doctor performs hand rub
  5. Drug preparation — doctor draws 0.50 mL at the supply cart, barcode check
  6. Aseptic site prep — doctor swabs the right deltoid
  7. Landmark & alignment — needle aligned to target (IK-corrected)
  8. Needle insertion — 90° IM, tip meets the deltoid surface exactly
  9. Dose delivery — plunger depresses, 0.50 mL over slow push
  10. Withdraw & safety — needle out, safety engaged
  11. Sharps disposal — syringe dropped in the yellow sharps container
  12. Observation & eSource — nurse documents; AE surveillance

High-DOF Robots & IK Needle Contact

  • 43 DOF per G1: 2×7-DOF arms, 2×7-DOF Dex3-1 hands with articulated finger segments that actually grip, 3-DOF waist, 2-DOF neck, 2×6-DOF legs with gait
  • Runtime inverse-kinematic tip correction drives the syringe needle tip onto the deltoid target (and 6 mm deep during dose) with zero geometry interpenetration — the v0.x object-overlap and needle-contact bugs are eliminated by construction
  • All props placed with verified clearances; coats are built into the torso shell (no overlay z-fighting)

Composite GCP Scoring

  • Time (30%) — total visit duration across all 12 phases
  • Accuracy (25%) — recorded needle placement deviation (mm)
  • Protocol adherence (20%) — deviations from the visit schedule
  • Sterility (10%) — aseptic technique index
  • Dose precision (10%) — delivered vs 0.500 mL target
  • Participant comfort (5%) — motion-jerk proxy

One station experiences a seeded Grade 1 adverse event (transient vasovagal episode) during observation: its ECG dips, the nurse responds, and the extended observation costs time — demonstrating autonomous AE surveillance and response.

PPO Policies

All stations run the same PPO architecture (MLP 64×64, tanh) with unique seeds (42, 137, 256, 512). Reward R = −0.3×time + 0.5/(1+dist) + 0.2/(1+jerk). Same policy, different state → distinct speed/accuracy/protocol trade-offs.

Why This Matters for Clinical Trials

Parallel autonomous stations executing the full GCP visit — identity, consent, vitals, dosing, AE surveillance, and eSource documentation — show how physical-AI sites could compress oncology trial timelines versus sequential human-staffed visits, while keeping a human CRA in the monitoring loop.
