Clinical Robot Competition v0.8.0

A 4-station clinical robot competition where SOTA Unitree G1 humanoid robots (from unitreerobotics) serve as doctors and nurses competing to perform patient injection procedures on realistic human patients. Premium dark theme with PBR-style materials, surgical overhead spotlights, glass observation window, medical curtain dividers, and animated vital signs. Each station runs independently with PPO RL policies.

SOTA Unitree G1 Robots

  • 1.32m tall, 35kg, 23–43 degrees of freedom
  • Dark charcoal body, glossy visor head, metallic joints
  • Dex3-1 hands with articulated finger segments
  • Battery pack on back, spine LED strip, ankle actuators
  • Doctor G1: white coat, red cross, syringe + alcohol swab
  • Nurse G1: blue coat, gold badge, tablet, stethoscope

Realistic Human Patients

  • Skin-colored body with facial features (eyes, nose, mouth)
  • Hospital wristband, pulse oximeter on finger
  • Visible veins on injection arm
  • Green hospital gown, proper seated posture

Competition Ranking

  • Time: Total elapsed seconds for all 7 phases
  • Accuracy: Needle-to-target distance. Score = 1/(1+dist*100)
  • Winner: Fastest total time, tiebroken by accuracy

Why Robot Competitions for Oncology

Having multiple autonomous robots compete simultaneously on injection procedures demonstrates that parallel robot trials can accelerate clinical workflows compared to sequential human trials, making future fully autonomous physical AI oncology trials faster.
